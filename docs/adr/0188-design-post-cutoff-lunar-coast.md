## Context and constraints

[R5.40](0187-longer-model-b-coast-passes.md) starts at planned ignition
and uses no thrust; its parking-coast agreement does not tell us what
happens after a burn. The new questions are whether two independent
integrators agree when restarted at an actual **simulated** cutoff,
and whether the spacecraft is moving away from the Moon at the
declared nodes of that coast. Numerical agreement and outward motion
have separate verdicts.

The original [0079 nominal cutoff](experiments/0079-research-reference.json)
is reproduced exactly by the [0098 baseline](experiments/0098-central-correction-study.json).
That baseline completed propagation but missed the terminal target.
The nominal/tighter cutoff difference already present in 0079 is
0.012750900510964487 m / 8.625915789265355e-6 m/s /
4.547473508864641e-13 kg. This is a separate incoming-state difference,
not part of the new same-start convergence comparison. The original
mass and boundary records are preserved.

## Options considered

- Extend the R5.40 parking coast or use an analytic hyperbola target as
  the achieved cutoff: neither input represents the end of the stored
  burn. The D14/D17 paths also use a different phase and force model,
  and their targeting did not close.
- Repeat the original full-force departure or solve for a new command:
  would revisit the burn and targeting before isolating this coast.
- Restart only model B from the fixed nominal cutoff: selected. Use the
  finished baseline boundary only, regardless of how the later trajectory
  behaves. This isolates the numerical question at a relevant state.

## Decision and rationale

Bind 0079 `science.nominal.boundaries[1]` and
`science.nominal.boundary_masses_kg[1]`, with exact equality to 0098
`science.runs[0].run` slot 1 and both departure burn records.
Use all stored SI position and velocity bits, SSB/J2000, at
978998315.9635216 TDB seconds since J2000 with **1351.7475727997967 kg**.
All four profiles start from that same cutoff; the end
978998915.9635216 TDB s is a comparison epoch, with no prescribed
endpoint state or sphere-of-influence target. The 13 coepochs are
cutoff plus 0, 50, ..., 600 s. Preserve the planned ignition and
cutoff distinction, original burn law and exact constant coast mass
as metadata. B uses six-state translation with zero thrust and
zero mass flow; it does not integrate a mass state.

This deliberately restarts a reduced model from a state generated by a
full-force run. It does not assert continuity of gravitational
acceleration between the two models. [ADR0135](0135-fixed-orbit-boundary-contract.md)
still requires one consistent force model at an actual mission interface.
The [D5](0082-identical-start-coast.md) and [D6](0083-coast-step-study.md)
full-force coast tests used this cutoff, but did not check the local
B coast with separate integrators.

Inherit the R5.40 Sun/Moon/Earth point fields, kernel order, pinned
environment, source and force checks, RK4 and DOP853 profiles, and
three same-start profile agreement gates at all 13 nodes. There
are 39 new comparisons and no parking-prefix comparison. The
independent stdlib-only audit checks arithmetic from saved operands
with no SPICE, scientific imports or solver. At each node, measure
Moon-relative radius, signed radial speed and osculating two-body
specific energy using one common saved Moon six-state. The audit
independently checks the radial and net-distance signs. Declare
sampled outward motion only with positive radial speed at all 52
profile nodes and positive net range change in all four profiles.
Zero or inward samples make outward motion unestablished even
when numerical agreement passes. Under B's moving Sun/Earth fields,
positive osculating lunar energy is neither an invariant nor a
lunar escape criterion.

## Evidence and verification

The [input manifest](experiments/0188-design-inputs.json) binds exact
0079 and 0098 cutoff records, the R1 candidate, historical
0079 cutoff differences, R5.40 results and tested inputs. The saved
Type 2 record intervals cover the entire proposed span strictly
inside the retained Sun/SSB, Earth/SSB and Moon/Earth intervals.
This is a metadata check; it does not measure source states on the
new arc. Static checks verify the boundary identity, original
burn epoch and mass arithmetic, hashes, links, scope and OpenSpec.
No SPICE, force or polynomial calls, native loads, trajectory
calculations or runtime tests were performed for the design.

## Consequences and revisit conditions

First prepare on artificial inputs, including inward, tangential
and outward motion and receipt/cap failure cases. After verified
preparation and separate authorization, allow one 60 s study with
at most two native arcs and two adaptive solves, and one 10 s
independent offline audit. Preserve finite complete or partial
evidence and stop on missing or failed work. No burn replay, model
A, new force model, retargeting, retry, shifted start or extended
window is authorized. A pass can establish local sampled agreement
and, separately, outward direction on that interval. It cannot
establish burn accuracy, continuous clearance, escape, a connection
to the interplanetary arc, mission safety or strict M3. Automation
remains paused.
