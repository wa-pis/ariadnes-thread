## Context and constraints

What problem requires a decision? Which requirements and constraints apply?
Separate known facts, assumptions and unknowns; do not invent prior rationale.

## Options considered

- Keep the current approach: benefits, costs and reason to retain/reject.
- Proposed alternative: benefits, costs and reason to select/reject.

List only actual alternatives considered; do not invent options to fill a quota.

## Decision and rationale

What was chosen, why, and within which boundaries? State what is out of scope.

## Consequences and revisit conditions

Benefits, drawbacks, risks, compatibility/migration effects and reversibility.
What measurable evidence or changed constraint would justify revisiting this?

## Evidence and verification

Link code, tests and retained experiments; identify measured revisions and units.
Distinguish observations from hypotheses and conditional mathematical claims.
State completed checks, pending checks and limitations. Decision acceptance alone
does not establish implementation completion, accuracy, safety or mission readiness.
