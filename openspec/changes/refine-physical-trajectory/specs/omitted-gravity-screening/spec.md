## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Freeze a diagnostic question before widening physical fidelity

R3 SHALL screen Earth/Moon/Mars local Sun-relative gravity at ADR0119 nodes0,60,120
under ADR0131, preserving all historical data and R1/R2/strict M3 gates. It SHALL
NOT propagate the body-centre endpoints or qualify a complete mission.

#### Scenario: Recognize inadmissible exterior force evaluations

- **WHEN** the retained endpoint position differences are audited without native calls
- **THEN** Moon node0 and Mars node120 SHALL classify inside their pinned exclusion spheres, with independent80-digit Decimal norm agreement within1e-18m
- **AND** this expected ideal-endpoint limitation SHALL NOT invalidate the Sun-only reference, be repaired by clipping/epsilon, or authorize new finite-radius boundary states

#### Scenario: Prepare an independent local differential-force diagnostic

- **WHEN** the private R3 verifier is prepared under ADR0131
- **THEN** it SHALL bind unchanged original/R2 hashes, exact three TDB epochs, SI/SSB/J2000 stored spacecraft/Sun states, original Moon/Mars endpoint states and GM, and ADR0131 radii/version/kernel identities
- **AND** body-relative direct-minus-indirect forces SHALL use the Sun as origin; each admitted component SHALL agree with a separate80-digit Decimal unit-vector/squared-distance implementation within max(1e-15m/s^2,1e-11*Decimal-vector-norm)
- **AND** independent analytic/rotation/translation/cancellation fixtures, wrong indirect-term signs, boundary-domain/metadata/identity/numeric/budget failures and explicit partial evidence SHALL pass native-free checks before execution

### Requirement: Bound local sampling without claiming trajectory accuracy

The R3 native screening SHALL occur once after verified preparation under one
cooperative30s budget and ADR0131's exact frozen acquisition order. A separate
offline audit SHALL recompute results without additional native reads.

#### Scenario: Obtain only missing authoritative inputs

- **WHEN** verified preparation permits the screening
- **THEN** a fresh pinned process SHALL read Earth GM at most once and at most seven exact-TDB SSB/J2000/NONE states in order Earth0,Mars0,Earth60,Moon60,Mars60,Earth120,Moon120, retaining raw states and attempted/completed counters
- **AND** no Sun requery, extra-body/GM read, search, spacecraft propagation, integration or resource substitution SHALL occur; initial/final original ordered13-kernel and source/runtime/build identities SHALL match
- **AND** setup/imports/pure checks/queries/arithmetic/inventories/evidence flush SHALL share30s with checks before/after calls; returned data and non-preemptible overrun SHALL be retained before failure publication

#### Scenario: Report partial domain coverage honestly

- **WHEN** a body term is inside/on its declared exclusion sphere
- **THEN** its acceleration SHALL be absent with distance/reason, never zero or a clipped force; endpoint rows0/120 SHALL have no three-body total or total ratio
- **AND** node60 SHALL require all three terms admissible before reporting individual signed/direct/indirect vectors, norms, vector sum, sum of individual norms and solar ratios; an unsupported middle-row domain SHALL stop unavailable
- **AND** no sample, ratio or arithmetic pass SHALL establish a whole-arc error bound, continuous safety, total omitted force, finite-burn feasibility, UI qualification or M3/M6 completion

#### Scenario: Preserve failure instead of widening the study

- **WHEN** a query, arithmetic, identity, unsupported mid-row domain or deadline gate fails unexpectedly
- **THEN** the verifier SHALL stop without retries or changed nodes/bodies/GM/epochs/gates/budget, preserve exclusive finite partial evidence and leave screening unverified
- **AND** if recovery requires a changed contract or user action, automation SHALL pause and identify the required decision; no conflicting automatic study SHALL launch
